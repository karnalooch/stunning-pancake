## Before you start

### How to prepare your phone?

1. Install the 4VELO app and sign in.
2. Grant permissions: **location always** (Android) / **Always** (iOS) — required for background tracking.
3. Disable battery optimization for the app (Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei — "unrestricted").
4. Charge before long activities; bring a power bank for rides >2 h.

### Do I need to keep the app open?

No. After starting a session you can lock the screen — GPS runs in background. On Android you'll see a "Tracking Active" notification.

## During activity

### Why does GPS "jump" or stop?

| Cause | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| Tunnel, garage, dense buildings | Normal — route fills in after open space |
| Phone in backpack | Move to side pocket or bike mount |
| Battery saver | Disable optimization for 4VELO |
| No signal >10 min | Finish activity; points sync later |

### Will I lose my route without internet?

**No.** The app stores points locally (outbox) and uploads when back online. After restart you may see a "complete sync" banner — accept and wait.

## After activity

### Why was my route rejected?

Anti-cheat checks physical movement parameters. Common reasons:

- Motor vehicle speed (e.g. driving).
- Route inconsistent with roads (cycling routing).
- Suspected GPS spoofing.

Details: [Anti-Cheat Policy](../trust/ANTI_CHEAT_SCORING_POLICY.md). You may **appeal** (e.g. downhill, GPS error).

### How long does verification take?

Usually a few minutes. Complex cases — up to 48 h. Status in training history.

---

## Battery

### How much does tracking use?

- Balanced mode (default): ~5–8% / hour (model-dependent).
- Tips: lower screen brightness; do not use airplane mode — GPS needs data.

### Strava / Garmin

You can connect in profile. Imported activities may be **flagged** — prize campaigns may require recording in the 4VELO app.
