## §4 Fair play — prohibited

1. **Motor vehicles** — car, motorcycle, e-scooter etc. with tracking enabled.
2. **GPS spoofing** — fake location apps or developer settings.
3. **Someone else's GPX / recording** — submitting another person's route.
4. **"Mule" account** — one person records km for another.
5. **Device manipulation** — shaking phone, motion simulators.
6. **Any other rule circumvention** harmful to fair competition.

Violations may result in: warning, activity exclusion, campaign exclusion, loss of prize.

## §5 Activity verification

1. Activities verified automatically (4-layer anti-cheat) and by moderators.
2. Organizer may require **native GPS from 4VELO app** for prize leaderboard eligibility.
3. Strava/Garmin import may be allowed with **lower weight** or informational only.
4. Activity status: verified / in queue / rejected / flagged — visible in app.
