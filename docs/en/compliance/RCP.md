## 3. Legal basis
- **Art. 6 section 1 letter b GDPR**: Processing necessary to perform the contract (activity tracking).
- **Art. 6 section 1 letter a GDPR**: User consent (marketing, biometric data).

## 4. Data Retention
- Activity data: Stored for the duration of the account + 1 year.
- Telemetry logs (raw): Deleted/anonymized after 90 days.
