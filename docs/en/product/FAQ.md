## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Why was my route rejected?
Our Anti-Cheat system checks the physical parameters of your movement. If the speed was too high (for example driving) or the route did not overlap with roads (for cycling), the activity may be rejected or flagged.

### 2. How do Privacy Zones work?
You can define zones (e.g. around the house) in which your GPS track will be automatically cut off or masked with noise. Thanks to this, your exact residential location will never be included in public rankings.

### 3. How to claim the prize?
After collecting the appropriate number of points (XP), go to the "Rewards" tab, select the voucher and click "Redeem". Show the generated QR code at the partner point.

## Tutorials (Quick instructions)

### First Gear/Ride
1. Open the app and wait for "GPS Lock".
2. Hold down the "START SESSION" button.
3. Put your phone away - the system works in the background.
4. When finished, hold "STOP" and wait for syncing.

### Garmin/Strava integration
1. Go to profile.
2. Select "Integrations".
3. Log in to your Garmin/Strava account and accept data transfer.
4. Your new activities will automatically land in 4VELO.

### 4. I lost my GPS signal while driving - will I lose my route?

The application saves the points locally (MMKV) and resends them when the network returns. After restarting the application, you may be asked to complete the shipment - accept and wait for synchronization. Details: [DATA_RESILIENCE.md](../../DATA_RESILIENCE.md).
